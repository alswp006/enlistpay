# EnlistPay - Design Context

## Users
20-something male soldiers (prospective enlistees and active service members) and their families who are Toss users. They need to consolidate military service tracking—discharge countdown, salary progression by rank, vacation accrual—into a single trusted source rather than checking multiple sites. The core job is reducing anxiety around timelines and financial clarity during mandatory military service.

## Brand Personality
Practical, transparent, steadfast. Tone is respectful and dependable—acknowledging the weight of military service while cutting through complexity. This app is a trusted companion, not casual or over-energetic. Aligned with Toss's clarity-first approach to financial information.

## Aesthetic Direction
TDS (Toss Design System) 준수. @toss/tds-mobile 컴포넌트 전용. 커스텀 미적 방향 없음.

## UX Principles
- **At-a-glance clarity**: D-day, current salary, vacation balance display without secondary navigation
- **Reliable calculation**: Soldiers must trust outputs (rank salary tables, discharge math, accrual logic) completely
- **Daily habit-friendly**: Widget and notification cadence support daily check-ins without friction
- **Transparent ad placement**: Reward gates on detailed earnings/simulation screens feel fair, not punitive
- **Linear information flow**: Enlistment date → discharge date → rank timeline → salary + vacation; no cognitive overhead
